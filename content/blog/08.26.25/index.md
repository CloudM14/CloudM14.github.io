## 08/26/25
 **Entry 3**

Over the past few weeks, I’ve been getting accustomed to a new level of understanding in my coursework. I’m currently retaking a couple of classes. Not because I struggled with the material before, but because this time around I’m approaching them with a deeper mindset. The theories that once felt abstract now click into place more intuitively, as if they’re the missing pieces of a puzzle I hadn’t fully seen before. It’s a reminder that learning isn’t always about racing ahead; sometimes it’s about circling back and building stronger foundations.

Beyond the classroom, I also joined the RPL (Rocket Propulsion Lab) and began hands-on work with the composites, DAQ, and analysis teams. Each group has been incredibly welcoming, no matter your level of knowledge or experience. It’s motivating to be in an environment where curiosity and willingness to learn are valued just as much as technical skill.

Lab Update

Over the past month, I worked on strain testing the main body of a solid rocket, helped with a nozzle and fin layup using a carbon fiber–like material, and ran a tensile strain test with analysis software. These projects gave me hands-on experience in both fabrication and structural testing.
